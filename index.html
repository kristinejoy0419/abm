How the score works

Drag to test weights. Rankings, stages, active evaluators, their committee coverage and actions update.

Fit 40%

  • AI maturity (60%)
  • Engineering hiring rate
  • AI and dev tools in use
  • Teams using them
  • Firmographics (40%)
  • Eng headcount, code platform, industry, region

Product usage 30%

  • Active seats
  • PRs reviewed per week
  • Repos connected
  • Teams on the product
  • Engineers using us on open source or personal projects
  • Today's PQA. Strong for mid-market.

Intent + engagement 30%

  • 6sense / Demandbase surge
  • G2 category and compare visits
  • Pricing and docs visits
  • Community, events, ads
  • Catches enterprise buyers who can't trial.

Score = Fit + Product usage + Intent, each times its weight. The three weights always add up to 100%, so raising one lowers the other two. Fit uses account traits only. Weights are tuned against closed-won deals. In this sample, AI Leaders win at 5x the rate of Late accounts.
